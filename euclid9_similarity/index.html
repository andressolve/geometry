Scaling up and down.

Same Shape, Different Size

Two triangles are similar if their corresponding angles are equal.

This ensures they have the exact same shape, just scaled differently.

Think of it like zooming in on a photo.

The picture gets bigger, but the contents don't get distorted.

Angles Stay the Same

Check the corners. When you zoom in, the angles stay exactly the same.

A right angle looks the same on a postage stamp and on a skyscraper.

Sides Scale Up

If one side gets twice as long, all sides must get twice as long.

You can't stretch just one side, or the shape would change!

"Everything grows together."

The Scale Factor

We call the multiplier the Scale Factor.

If the scale factor is 2, every side becomes 2 times as long.

New Length = Original Length × Scale Factor

Observation: Ratios are preserved.

If a, b are sides of one triangle and x, y are corresponding sides of the other:

a / b = x / y

(The internal ratio of sides stays the same)

Finding Missing Sides

We use the scale factor to find missing lengths. It's just multiplication.

1. Find the pair of matching sides with numbers (2 and 6).

2. Calculate the multiplier (Scale Factor is 3).

3. Multiply the other side by that factor.
