What is a Circle?

A circle is all points that are the same distance from a center point.

This distance is called the radius.

Every point on the circle is exactly one radius away from the center.

In the diagram, all the lines from the center to the circle have the same length.

Triangles from the Center

Pick any two points on the circle: A and B.

Connect them to the center O.

Triangle OAB has two sides that are radii.

Since all radii are equal: OA = OB

This is an isosceles triangle!

Remember isosceles triangles from Lesson 6?

The Theorem

∠AOB = 2 ∠ACB

The Proof

Given: Circle with center O. Points A, B, C on the circle. ∠ACB = c.
Prove: ∠AOB = 2c
Statement
Reason

8. ∠AOB = ∠AOD + ∠BOD = 2a + 2b
Angle addition
9. ∠AOB = 2(a + b) = 2c
