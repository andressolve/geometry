Slope

Have a look at y = 2x. Start at any point on it, move 1 right and 2 up, and you land right back on the line.

Now have a look at y = 3x. Start at any point on it, move 1 right and 3 up, and you land right back on the line.

Those numbers — 2, 3 — describe something special about these lines. They are called the slope.

The slope of a line is how much y increases when x increases by 1.

What makes a line a line?

The same step, everywhere. Move 1 to the right, and y changes by the same amount — no matter where you start. That amount is the slope.
