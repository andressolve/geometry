What We Discovered

Some points on the coordinate plane line up — they form a line.

When points line up, their coordinates follow a rule — a relationship between x and y.

We can write this rule as an equation, like y = 2x or y = x + 1.

Every point on the line satisfies the equation. Every point that satisfies the equation is on the line.
